## Model output

## Coffee Chat Prep

**Quick context:** Ayesha is a senior leader managing a massive, high-velocity product in a highly regulated market; she'll appreciate a peer-level discussion about the trade-offs of returning to India rather than a standard "how do I get hired" conversation.

**Opening line:** It’s great to connect, and I’ve been thinking a lot about the evolution of India's digital payment ecosystem since my time at Block.

**Questions to ask:**
1. You've navigated the scale of a platform like PhonePe while UPI regulations have tightened—how have these external constraints actually shifted your team's product roadmap or experimentation speed? *Why this works: It shows you understand the regulatory pressure on Indian fintechs and moves the conversation toward strategic decision-making.*
2. I’m weighing a return to an operator role at a market leader versus moving into the India-focused VC space; from your seat, what's the biggest "skill delta" that you feel is harder to sharpen in a fund role compared to leading product here? *Why this works: It frames your career dilemma as a genuine desire to maximize your professional growth, which senior leaders love to mentor on.*
3. Having worked in US fintech, I’m curious about the trade-offs—what’s one nuance of the Indian consumer’s behavior that you think is most misunderstood by those who haven't built products in this market? *Why this works: It positions you as someone with relevant experience who is humble enough to recognize the local complexity.*
4. If you were looking at your next five years, would you prioritize staying close to the product "build" or moving closer to the "capital allocation" side of the ecosystem? *Why this works: It forces her to reflect on her own career trajectory and gives you a window into how she values her current role.*
5. What’s a "hard truth" about being a product leader at a company of PhonePe’s size that you didn't fully appreciate until you were in the role? *Why this works: It invites the "unfiltered" answer that is much more valuable than a curated corporate response.*

**If the conversation stalls:**
1. How do you see the next wave of financial inclusion evolving beyond just payments in the Indian market?
2. What’s the biggest challenge you’re currently facing in maintaining product velocity while managing such a large team?

---

**Heads up:** Since Ayesha is a Head of Product, she’ll be looking for your ability to think critically about market dynamics rather than just your resume. Avoid asking for an internship or job directly; if you demonstrate high-level thinking, she’ll likely offer to connect you with the right person on her team or in her network herself.
