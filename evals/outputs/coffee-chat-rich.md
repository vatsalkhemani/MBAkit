**Opening line:** Thanks for taking the time, especially since I'm trying to weigh whether to build my product muscles at a foundation model shop or an application-layer startup.

**Questions to ask:**
1. As someone sitting at the foundation model layer, how do you distinguish between a "real" product insight and just riding the wave of underlying model capability improvements? *Why this works: It challenges him to define the core value of his role, which shows you’re thinking about the substance of PM work rather than just the prestige of the company.*

3. When you look at the landscape, where do you think a PM gains more durable judgment: navigating the constraints of a foundation model or solving the distribution and UX challenges at the application layer? *Why this works: It frames your career dilemma as a strategic industry question, which is more engaging for a Principal PM than a personal "what should I do" question.*
4. What’s one assumption about the "AI PM" role that you held before joining Anthropic that you’ve had to completely discard since being on the inside? *Why this works: It’s a low-pressure way to get a candid, non-rehearsed answer about the reality of his day-to-day.*

Given your vantage point, how do you see the role of the PM evolving as these models become more commoditized and the competitive moat shifts toward domain-specific data? *Why this works: It shows you’re paying attention to the long-term industry trajectory rather than just trying to land a job.*

**If the conversation stalls:** 
1. "What’s the most surprising way your team uses the models you’re building to actually improve your own internal product processes?"
2. "If you were stepping into an MBA program today, what’s the one skill you’d double down on to prepare for a PM role in this specific sector?"
