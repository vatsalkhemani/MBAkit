## User message sent

```
Sharpen these resume bullets:

- Analyzed 12 public companies across industrials sector using DCF, comps, and LBO models, produced 30-page investment thesis
- Built proprietary screening tool in Python that flagged 8 acquisition targets for MD team, 2 of which became live deals
- Presented weekly industry update to 15-person sector team, including senior bankers and 3 MDs

Target role/industry: Private equity (buy-side)
Goal: Summer internship at a middle-market PE fund

Analyze each bullet against the diagnosis checklist. Provide all three versions for each. Follow the output format exactly.
```

## Model output

### Bullet 1
**Original:** Analyzed 12 public companies across industrials sector using DCF, comps, and LBO models, produced 30-page investment thesis

**Diagnosis:**
- WEAK VERB: "Analyzed" and "produced" are low-impact verbs that describe effort rather than value.
- BURIED LEAD: The technical rigor is clear, but the investment outcome or decision-making influence is missing.
- NO NUMBERS: While you have the number of companies, you lack the financial impact or the outcome of the thesis (e.g., was it approved, did it inform a bid?).

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Evaluated 12 industrial companies through DCF, LBO, and comps analysis to support investment decision-making.
*What changed: Swapped generic "analyzed" for "evaluated" and reframed the work as supporting a decision.*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Authored 30-page investment thesis on [Industry] sector, utilizing DCF and LBO modeling to pressure-test valuation for senior leadership.
*What changed: Shifted focus to the output (thesis) and the purpose (pressure-testing valuation).*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Spearheaded comprehensive valuation of 12 industrial targets, producing a 30-page investment thesis that informed a $[X]M bid recommendation for the [Investment Committee/MDs].
*What changed: Used a power verb ("Spearheaded") and added a placeholder for the financial impact of the work.*

### Bullet 2
**Original:** Built proprietary screening tool in Python that flagged 8 acquisition targets for MD team, 2 of which became live deals

**Diagnosis:**
- STRONG: Good use of "Built" and clear quantification of outcomes.
- BURIED LEAD: The technical efficiency (Python) is good, but the value is in the deal flow generation.

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Built a proprietary Python screening tool to identify 8 high-potential acquisition targets, resulting in 2 active live deals.
*What changed: Streamlined the phrasing for better flow and impact.*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Engineered a proprietary Python screening tool that automated deal sourcing, identifying 8 targets and accelerating 2 into active live deal processes.
*What changed: Used "Engineered" and "automated" to highlight the technical efficiency and speed.*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Architected a proprietary Python screening engine that reduced sourcing time by [X]% and generated 2 live deals with a combined enterprise value of $[X]M.
*What changed: Added placeholders for time-saved and deal-size metrics to demonstrate bottom-line impact.*

### Bullet 3
**Original:** Presented weekly industry update to 15-person sector team, including senior bankers and 3 MDs

**Diagnosis:**
- WEAK VERB: "Presented" is a low-impact verb; it casts you as a messenger rather than an influencer.
- VAGUE IMPACT: What was the result of these updates? Did they drive deal flow? Did they inform the strategy?
- BURIED LEAD: The "senior bankers and 3 MDs" is the most important part (visibility), but it's hidden at the end.

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Delivered weekly industry updates to 15-person sector team, including 3 MDs, to align the team on market trends and deal opportunities.
*What changed: Clarified the purpose of the update (alignment on market trends).*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Briefed 3 MDs and a 15-person team weekly on industrial sector developments, facilitating data-driven discussions on potential market entries.
*What changed: Used "Briefed" and "facilitated" to imply a more active role in the team's strategic discourse.*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Influenced sector deal strategy by briefing 3 MDs and 15 team members on weekly market shifts, directly contributing to [X] new investment opportunities.
*What changed: Positioned yourself as an influencer of strategy rather than a presenter of news.*

---

**Overall patterns:** You have strong raw materials but lean toward describing tasks rather than outcomes. For PE, senior partners care about two things: how you save them time and how you help them make money. Shift your language from "I did this task" to "I provided this insight that allowed the MDs to make a decision."
