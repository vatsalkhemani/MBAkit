# resume-sparse

**Tool:** resume
**Mode:** sparse
**Label:** Vague bullets with no metrics
**Latency:** 5075ms

---

## User message sent

```
Sharpen these resume bullets:

- Responsible for managing social media for a nonprofit
- Helped with customer research for a startup
- Worked on the onboarding flow redesign

No specific industry. Give general improvements.
Goal: Land a tech PM internship

Analyze each bullet against the diagnosis checklist. Provide all three versions for each. Follow the output format exactly.
```

---

## Model output

### Bullet 1
**Original:** Responsible for managing social media for a nonprofit

**Diagnosis:**
- WEAK VERB: "Responsible for managing" is a soft, non-committal phrase that minimizes your ownership.
- NO NUMBERS: Lacks context on audience size, engagement rates, or growth metrics.
- VAGUE IMPACT: Does not explain how social media contributed to the nonprofit's goals (e.g., donations, awareness).

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Directed social media strategy and content creation for a nonprofit, increasing total audience engagement by [X]%.
*What changed: Swapped passive "responsible for" with the active verb "Directed" and added a placeholder for engagement metrics.*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Executed a multi-channel social media strategy, growing follower base by [X]% and driving [Y] clicks to the donation landing page.
*What changed: Shifted focus from "managing" to "executing a strategy" and added placeholders for growth and conversion metrics.*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Architected a social media growth campaign that expanded brand reach by [X]% and increased monthly donor conversions by [Y]% over [timeframe].
*What changed: Used "Architected" to demonstrate strategic ownership and linked social media output to tangible organizational outcomes.*

---

### Bullet 2
**Original:** Helped with customer research for a startup

**Diagnosis:**
- WEAK VERB: "Helped with" is the weakest possible verb; it implies you were a bystander rather than a driver.
- NO NUMBERS: No indication of the scope of research (number of interviews, size of survey) or the impact on product direction.
- VAGUE IMPACT: Does not state how this research influenced the startup's product or business strategy.

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Conducted customer research interviews to identify user pain points and inform product feature prioritization.
*What changed: Replaced "Helped with" with the specific action "Conducted" and defined the purpose of the research.*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Synthesized insights from [X] customer interviews to identify [Y] key friction points, directly influencing the product roadmap for [feature name].
*What changed: Added placeholders for volume and specific impact, demonstrating how research translates into product strategy.*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Led customer discovery for [Product Name], conducting [X] interviews to validate product-market fit, which resulted in a [Y]% reduction in churn-risk features.
*What changed: Framed the research as a leadership activity ("Led") and quantified the business impact of the findings.*

---

### Bullet 3
**Original:** Worked on the onboarding flow redesign

**Diagnosis:**
- WEAK VERB: "Worked on" is non-committal and fails to describe your specific contribution (design, implementation, or analysis).
- NO NUMBERS: No mention of before/after metrics, time saved, or conversion improvement.
- VAGUE IMPACT: "Redesign" is a process, not an outcome. What did the redesign actually achieve?

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Redesigned the user onboarding flow to simplify the registration process and improve user completion rates.
*What changed: Replaced "Worked on" with the strong verb "Redesigned" and clarified the objective.*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Optimized the onboarding flow by [action taken], resulting in a [X]% increase in user activation within the first 24 hours.
*What changed: Added a placeholder for the specific method used and a key performance metric for a PM role.*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Spearheaded an end-to-end onboarding redesign, reducing time-to-value by [X]% and increasing day-30 user retention by [Y] percentage points.
*What changed: Used "Spearheaded" to show ownership and included two critical PM success metrics: time-to-value and retention.*

---

**Overall patterns:** You consistently use low-impact, non-committal verbs like "Worked on," "Helped with," and "Responsible for." For a PM role, you must lead with your impact. Always quantify your work using the [X], [Y], [Z] framework—specifically focusing on metrics like conversion rates, user retention, and time-to-value.
