# cold-email-rich

**Tool:** cold-email
**Mode:** rich
**Label:** Rich input — personal detail, career-switcher context
**Latency:** 25624ms

---

## User message sent

```
Write a cold email with these details:

Sender: Priya Patel, Wharton MBA '28
Recipient: Marcus Liu, Director of Product at Notion
Connection: LinkedIn cold
Goal: Explore PM roles post-MBA and learn about Notion's API platform direction
Specific detail about them: He gave a talk at Figma Config 2025 on building a developer ecosystem around no-code tools. I'm switching from biotech R&D and want to understand how domain experts become product leaders.
Tone: warm

Write the email now. Follow the structure and format exactly. The email must be immediately sendable with no placeholders or brackets.
```

---

## Model output

**Subject:** Wharton MBA / your Config 2025 talk on Notion’s ecosystem

Hi Marcus,

Your talk at Config 2025 on building a developer ecosystem around no-code tools really stuck with me. I’m currently transitioning from biotech R&D into product and have been thinking a lot about how domain experts successfully pivot into platform leadership roles.

I’d love to hear how you navigated that transition at Notion and get your perspective on where the API platform is heading. Would you have 20 minutes for a brief call next week? I'm happy to send over a few questions beforehand to make the best use of your time.

Best,
Priya Patel
Wharton MBA '28

---

**Why this works:**
- Opens with a specific, high-signal reference to his recent talk, proving the sender has done their homework.
- Bridges the gap between the sender's biotech background and the recipient's role, framing the transition as a thoughtful exploration of leadership rather than a generic job search.
- Makes the ask low-friction by offering to send questions in advance, which respects his time as a Director.
