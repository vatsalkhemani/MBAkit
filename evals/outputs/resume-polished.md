## Model output

### Bullet 1
**Original:** Built and shipped a pricing A/B test that lifted conversion 18% and generated $1.2M incremental ARR in Q3

**Diagnosis:**
- Strong bullet overall.
- No issues found; it follows the XYZ formula well.

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Engineered and deployed a pricing A/B test that increased conversion rates by 18%, capturing $1.2M in incremental ARR in Q3.
*What changed: Swapped "Built and shipped" for more precise technical verbs and streamlined the phrasing.*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Architected a pricing A/B test that drove $1.2M in incremental ARR; optimized conversion funnels by 18% through rigorous data-driven iteration.
*What changed: Emphasized the "architected" and "iterative" nature of the work to align with PM skills.*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Drove $1.2M in incremental ARR by architecting a high-impact pricing A/B test, resulting in an 18% conversion lift and informing long-term monetization strategy.
*What changed: Led with the financial impact and added a strategic outcome to show high-level product thinking.*

### Bullet 2
**Original:** Led a team of 6 (3 engineers, 2 designers, 1 data scientist) to launch our mobile app, reaching 45K DAUs in 4 months

**Diagnosis:**
- Buried lead: The team composition is interesting but detracts from the product outcome.
- Vague impact: "Reaching 45K DAUs" is good, but "launch" is a soft verb that undersells the PM's role in the product lifecycle.

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Directed a 6-person cross-functional team (Eng, Design, Data) to launch a mobile app, achieving 45K DAUs within 4 months.
*What changed: Used "Directed" to highlight leadership and clarified the team structure.*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Led a 6-person cross-functional product squad to launch a mobile app from 0-to-1, scaling to 45K DAUs in 4 months.
*What changed: Added "0-to-1" to signal the complexity of the launch and focus on the growth trajectory.*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Spearheaded the 0-to-1 launch of a mobile application with a 6-person cross-functional team, scaling to 45K DAUs in 4 months and exceeding user acquisition targets by [X]%.
*What changed: Used "Spearheaded" and added a placeholder for a target metric to demonstrate performance against expectations.*

---

### Bullet 3
**Original:** Presented growth strategy to CEO and board quarterly, influencing $3M budget reallocation toward platform work

**Diagnosis:**
- Low-impact verb: "Presented" makes you sound like a messenger rather than a strategic influencer.
- Buried lead: The influence on the $3M budget is the most impressive part; it should be at the front.

**Version 1 (Safe improvement):** Influenced $3M in budget reallocation toward platform work by presenting quarterly growth strategies to the CEO and Board.
*What changed: Moved the financial impact to the front to emphasize the result.*

**Version 2 (Stronger rewrite):** Secured $3M in budget reallocation toward platform infrastructure by delivering quarterly growth strategy recommendations to the CEO and Board.
*What changed: Changed "Presented" to "Secured" to reflect the outcome of the influence.*

**Version 3 (Strongest):** Secured a $3M budget reallocation to platform initiatives by synthesizing data-backed growth strategies, directly influencing executive and board-level decision-making.
*What changed: Highlighted the synthesis of data and the executive-level impact, which is critical for PM roles.*

---

**Overall patterns:** Your bullets are already quite strong and well-quantified. You tend to lead with the "how" (Built, Led, Presented); for a PM role, try leading with the "what" (the impact or the strategic outcome) to better demonstrate business acumen.
