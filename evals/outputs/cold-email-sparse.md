# cold-email-sparse

**Tool:** cold-email
**Mode:** sparse
**Label:** Minimal input — just name, role, company, alumni connection
**Latency:** 17376ms

---

## User message sent

```
Write a cold email with these details:

Sender: Vatsal Khemani, Wharton MBA '28
Recipient: Sarah Chen, Senior PM at Stripe
Connection: Alumni
Goal: Coffee chat about PM career
No personal detail provided. Use ONLY their role and company to craft the connection. Do NOT invent specific articles, talks, or career moves.
Tone: warm

Write the email now. Follow the structure and format exactly. The email must be immediately sendable with no placeholders or brackets.
```

---

## Model output

**Subject:** Wharton MBA / quick question about PM at Stripe

Hi Sarah,

Fellow Wharton alum here, found your profile through the directory. I'm currently exploring product management roles and Stripe's approach to scaling payments infrastructure has been on my radar as I evaluate potential career paths.

Would you have 20 minutes for a brief call next week to hear about your experience? I'm happy to work around your schedule.

Best,
Vatsal Khemani
Wharton MBA '28

---

**Why this works:**
- Opens with a clear credibility signal (alumni status) without using the banned "I'm a student" opener.
- Frames the interest around the company's work rather than making up fake personal details, which maintains professional integrity.
- Keeps the ask specific and low-commitment by requesting a defined 20-minute window.
