# cold-email-boutique-vp

**Tool:** cold-email
**Mode:** rich
**Label:** Cold email to VP at a boutique firm (not FAANG/unicorn), career switcher from healthcare
**Latency:** 9119ms

---

## User message sent

```
Write a cold email with these details:

Sender: Sneha Reddy, Wharton MBA '28
Recipient: David Alvarez, VP of Strategy at Welch Health Advisors (boutique healthcare consulting, ~60 people)
Connection: Wharton alum
Goal: Explore healthcare-focused consulting post-MBA
Specific detail about them: He wrote a piece in Health Affairs last fall on value-based care reimbursement models and mentioned at our recruiting event that his firm turned down a PE acquisition offer last year to preserve independence.
Tone: professional

Write the email now. Follow the structure and format exactly. The email must be immediately sendable with no placeholders or brackets.
```

---

## Model output

**Subject:** Wharton alum / question on Welch Health Advisors' independence

Hi David,

Fellow Wharton alum here, found your profile through our directory and have been following Welch Health Advisors' work in the boutique healthcare space. Your piece in Health Affairs last fall on value-based care reimbursement models was a great read, and I was struck by your point at the recruiting event about turning down a PE acquisition to preserve the firm's independence. 

I’m currently exploring healthcare-focused consulting roles and would love to hear your perspective on the trade-offs of that model. Would you have 20 minutes for a brief call next week?

Best,
Sneha Reddy
Wharton MBA '28

---

**Why this works:**
- Opens with a clear, credible connection (alumni status) without using the banned "I'm a student" opener.
- Weaves in two specific details—his article and his strategic decision regarding the firm's independence—which demonstrates genuine research and interest in his specific firm.
- Makes a low-friction, time-bound ask that is easy for a busy VP to agree to.
