# cold-email-referral

**Tool:** cold-email
**Mode:** rich
**Label:** Referral-based cold email (mutual connection, not alumni/LinkedIn)
**Latency:** 5078ms

---

## User message sent

```
Write a cold email with these details:

Sender: James Chen, Wharton MBA '28
Recipient: Maya Rodriguez, Head of Growth at Ramp
Connection: Referral (Introduced through Alex Park (ex-Ramp PM, now classmate of mine))
Goal: Informational chat about growth-PM roles at fintech
Specific detail about them: Alex mentioned you rebuilt Ramp's activation funnel last year and that you're the person who'd have the sharpest take on growth PM work in B2B fintech.
Tone: warm

Write the email now. Follow the structure and format exactly. The email must be immediately sendable with no placeholders or brackets.
```

---

## Model output

**Subject:** Alex Park suggested I reach out / Growth PM

Hi Maya,

Alex Park suggested I reach out to you as I explore growth PM roles in the B2B fintech space. Alex mentioned you rebuilt Ramp's activation funnel last year and that you’d have the sharpest take on how to approach growth in this sector.

Would you have 20 minutes for a brief call next week to share your perspective on the space? I’m happy to work around your schedule.

Best,
James Chen
Wharton MBA '28

---

**Why this works:**
- It uses a high-trust referral (Alex Park) as the hook, which guarantees the email gets opened.
- It validates Maya’s specific expertise by referencing the activation funnel project, showing the sender has done their homework and values her unique experience.
- It keeps the ask low-friction and specific (20 minutes, next week), making it easy for a busy executive to say yes.
